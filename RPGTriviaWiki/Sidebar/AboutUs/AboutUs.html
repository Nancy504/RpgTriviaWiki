About Us


We're a team who are extremely passionate about Role Playing Games and love researching about the different types of games all around the world. This space is intended to share the love that was placed in these games and stories.


This website contains all the classic RPG games both new and old and some fun facts that aren't as known as before. We are open to collaborate with anyone who understands the language of the original sources for researching facts, anyone who is willing to fact check the information we gathered, or anyone who wants to make a page, and anyone who is willing to share a rpg game they would like to add in the website.


We also encourage people to use the discussion board to either discuss the game or ways to help improve the website or information that was displayed. Anything inappropriate, malicious, politically, or disrespectful posted on the discussion board will be warned, have their account disabled for a certain amount of time, and finally permanently banned. Also make sure to hide your spoilers using the spoiler tag. Anyone who accidentally spoils will only get warnings but those that intentionally spoil to annoy others will get timed out and if this behaviour continues then a ban will be placed.


We understand that games can invoke vulnerable memories, trauma and emotions for people. Those kinds of discussions are not allowed in the discussion for both your safety and for anyone else who may be triggered from the post. Please have that kind of conversation with a loved one, through therapy, or use the resources we have listed in the social and support page.


Thank you to everyone who has visited this site and those that have helped with the research and pages. There is nothing more satisfying than sharing a hobby that you can deeply be invested in.
